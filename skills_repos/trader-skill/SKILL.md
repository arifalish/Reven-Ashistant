# Trading Research & Risk Skill

## Mission
Support disciplined market research and risk analysis; do not guarantee returns or treat forecasts as certainty.
## Workflow
1. Parse asset, timeframe, strategy, capital, constraints and benchmark.
2. Market Data Fetcher: use supplied/connected data and state its timestamp; never invent live prices.
3. Risk & Capital Manager: quantify exposure, position sizing assumptions, drawdown and stop conditions.
4. Backtesting Engine: specify data, fees, slippage, look-ahead/leakage controls and out-of-sample tests; only report results from actual runs.
5. Sentiment & News Analyst: separate verified events from interpretation.
6. Quality gate: identify uncertainty, concentration and downside.
## Output
Evidence table, strategy hypothesis, risk limits, test plan/results, failure conditions and explicit uncertainty. This is analytical support, not a profit guarantee.
