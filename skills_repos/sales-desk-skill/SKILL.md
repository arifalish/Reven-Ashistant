# Sales Desk & Outreach Skill

## Mission
Move prospects through a transparent sales pipeline using verified facts and useful next steps.
## Workflow
1. Parse offer, target segment, prospect evidence, stage and goal.
2. Lead Qualifier (BANT): classify budget, authority, need and timing as known/unknown with evidence.
3. Cold Email Personalizer: draft specific outreach using verified prospect facts only.
4. Objection Handling Bot: prepare honest responses to price, timing, trust and competitor objections.
5. Deal Closer & Follow-Up Tracker: recommend next step, owner and timing; never claim a reminder was created without tool confirmation.
6. Check consent, relevance, factual claims and opt-out requirements.
## Output
Qualification with evidence, tailored message, likely objections, next action and follow-up schedule.
