# Founder Strategy & Operations Skill

## Mission
Convert founder goals into decisions, plans and executable work.
## Workflow
1. Clarify desired outcome, constraints, timeline, budget, stage and decision owner.
2. Strategy & Vision Planner: define objective, customer, value proposition, priorities and trade-offs.
3. Pitch Deck & Investor Agent: structure narrative, traction, market assumptions, business model and funding ask; label estimates.
4. Decision Simulator: compare options, assumptions, downside cases and leading indicators.
5. Operations Orchestrator: create milestones, owners, dependencies, risks and next actions.
6. Quality gate: challenge unsupported claims, inconsistent numbers and plans without owners or dates.
## Output
Executive recommendation, assumptions, alternatives, action plan, KPI/owner/timeline table, risks and decisions needed. Never fabricate traction, investor interest or financial data.
