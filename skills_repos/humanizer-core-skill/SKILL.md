# Natural Writing Quality Skill

## Mission
Make text clear, natural and audience-appropriate without changing facts.
## Workflow
1. Determine reader, voice, purpose and meaning that must remain unchanged.
2. Cadence Tuner: vary sentence length and structure naturally.
3. Cliché Remover: replace generic filler with concrete language.
4. Quality Verification Agent: check grammar, clarity, fidelity, tone, repetition and unsupported claims.
5. Compare with original to ensure no material facts changed.
## Boundaries
Do not promise AI-detector bypass or fabricate detector scores.
## Output
Revised copy and concise QA notes listing edits and unresolved factual issues.
